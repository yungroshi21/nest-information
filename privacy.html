Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal Nest integration operated by the household owner and this informational website.

Information accessed

When authorized by the owner, the integration accesses selected Nest device identifiers, basic device information, settings, temperature setpoints, temperature and humidity sensor readings, operating state and supported device events. Authorized home information can include home name, room names and postal code. Google OAuth credentials and tokens enable the connection.

Purpose and processing

Information is used to display and control the owner's thermostat in Home Assistant and support household automations. Google processes authorization and Nest data; Google Cloud Pub/Sub carries device updates. Home Assistant stores credentials, configuration and device information in the owner's private installation. Configured history, logs and backups may retain copies.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train general-purpose AI models. This integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access and sharing

The owner and people the owner authorizes to use Home Assistant may access information according to their permissions. Data is processed by Google and the owner's Home Assistant system to provide the integration. This website does not display thermostat readings, collect Google credentials or connect directly to the private installation.

Retention and deletion

Device history and logs are retained according to the owner's Home Assistant configuration. Backups follow the owner's backup retention settings. Removing the integration stops its ongoing access; historical records and backups may require separate deletion. The owner can revoke Google authorization in their Google account's third-party connections settings.

Website and contact

This static website contains no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or application cookies. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to operate and secure hosting. Household users can contact the owner through their existing household communication channel for access or deletion requests.
